## Match score, 0–100

Domain overlap 40 · level and scope 20 · sector 15 · location 15 (preferred city or remote/hybrid 15, secondary city 5) · company quality 10. Score 70+ goes to the main feed, 50–69 to Maybe, below 50 is dropped but logged.

Change the weights or cut-offs if they don't suit you; the scan reads them from here.
